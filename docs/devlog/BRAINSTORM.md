## Q16. The release for everyone, before or after phases 7-9

Phase 6 is the release meant for everyone, 1.0.0. Phases 7-9 (the
readers on the live pages, artifacts, more webapps) came after it in
number, but the live look found that ChatGPT's reader and long chats
need them to work well.

- **a)** Phases 7-9 first; phase 6 last, 1.0.0 when the readers are
  verified. Versions go 0.7.x, 0.8.x, 0.9.x meanwhile.
- **b)** Phase 6 now with what works (Claude, AI Mode, Copilot), phases
  7-9 as 1.x.

*Lean:* a). A 1.0.0 whose ChatGPT reader reads one message in four
would teach its first users the extension does not work.
