## The gate's `close`

`open` remembers its tabs in `storage.local` (`storage.session` is emptied by each reload of the extension), forgotten when Firefox starts; `close` (one tabId, or none: all of them)
shuts only those. Tabs opened before the tool existed are not known to it.

## The long ChatGPT chat, to its start

Five runs of the whole read on the same long chat (counts only; its text is Sett's):

| Run | What changed | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | as it was | 750 messages, 14 different, 31 min: the last 10 again and again |
| 2 | keyed by `data-chatgpt-search-message-ids` | 12 in 80 s: stepping up stood still, ChatGPT pulls the view back to the end |
| 3 | steps from the end, a pulled view put back | 734 in 15 min (433 pulls) |
| 4 | a window meeting nothing: stepped back down | 224, ended by patience: too slow |
| 5 | instead read again after a longer pause | 734 in 17 min; fresh tab: 736 in 17 min |

- ChatGPT's unit key on a long chat is `fallback-turn-<n>:<n>:<role>`, `<n>` the turn's
  place among those loaded: it changes as older ones load. The message ids stay.
- Scrolled up, ChatGPT puts the view back to the end, about every other step.
- At its top it loads older ones on a move there, after a second or two, and puts the
  view at the end again.
- One fresh tab ended at once with the 10 first loaded; the next fresh tab read all. A box
  the page replaced would do that (one out of the page is always at its top): the read
  now finds its box again when it left the page. Not seen to happen.
