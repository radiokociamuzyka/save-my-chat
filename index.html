save-my-chat

Your AI chats, saved on your own machine.

A Firefox extension that takes a conversation out of a chat webapp and saves it as Markdown or JSON, in one format that reads the same whichever service it came from. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Download v0.4.15 How to install it

Not on addons.mozilla.org yet: for now it is loaded as a temporary add-on, and the manual says how.

How it works

  1. Open a conversation in one of the webapps below.
  2. Click the toolbar button. It says which reader sees the page and how many messages it found.
  3. Choose Markdown or JSON. The file lands in your downloads.

Nothing leaves your machine

The extension reads only the page you have open, and only when you click. It writes files through Firefox's downloads, and makes no network requests of its own: no telemetry, no accounts, no remote code. What it reads and writes.
