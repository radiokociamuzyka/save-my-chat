Readers

Each chat webapp has its own reader. Turn off the ones you do not want injected.

Webapps rename their classes with their builds. When a reader stops seeing messages (the popup's "What the reader saw" tells which part), put a CSS selector that finds them under that part, one per line: yours are tried before the built-in ones and take effect on the next save, no update needed.

    Library

    A copy of each chat you save is kept here, in Firefox's storage for this extension, so you can find it, save it again, and see in the popup when it grew since. It never leaves your machine; Forget removes it.

      Prototypes

      A prototype is your own copy of a reader: change its code, and it runs in place of the default on the pages you name, until you turn it off or delete it. Nothing leaves your machine; export them to keep or share.
